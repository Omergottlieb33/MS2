1Samples and stages

2Cells: tracking filters

A cell is analysed when it is tracked through enough of its stage window and its segmented volume is stable (volume CV = standard deviation over mean, over its tracked frames). Only these cells are analysed; every segmented cell is still shown when drawing the region and the segments below.

3Region and segments

One panel per sample, turned as set above. Pick a segment (2–5), click around it, then close; outline each on the start, middle and end frame (any of them; the middle frame is set in the sample above). A cell is in a segment when it lies inside its outline on that frame. The ROI (white) is the segments joined: a cell is analysed when it passes the tracking filters and lies in any segment (no segment: every cell passing the filters). Once segments 2–5 are all outlined, a table beside the panel gives the cells each will analyse. On the segment grid a cell sits at its average position over the window; the dashed box is what the grid is cut from. Nothing is drawn from earlier work unless you load a segment or region file for the sample.

4Activity clustering

A cell fires on a frame when its MS2 signal is above the noise floor. Cells whose highest signal stays below the blip height are short bursts / noise; the rest are split into weakly, moderate and highly active by their rate (signal above the floor per tracked frame). Automatic places the two rate cuts where KMeans puts them on all samples' kept cells together; moving a cut takes over by hand.

Heatmap

Rows: every cell above silent, highest level on top (red lines between levels), then by sample, then by rate. Columns: the position in each sample's stage window, 0 = its start and 1 = its end, so windows of different lengths line up; grey where a cell is not tracked. Colour: signal on a log scale, the floor and below at the bottom.

5Level composition and spatial map

The share of each sample's analysed cells in every activity level, silent cells included, and every sample's analysed cells, turned as set above and coloured by level (+ = the centre of the analysed cells): at their position on the start, middle or end frame, with that frame's ROI and segment outlines, or at their average position over the window, as the exported spatial_maps_aligned. Both follow every setting above.

Active cells over time

Per sample, over its stage window (0 = its start, 1 = its end, so windows of different lengths line up): active at the frame -- the % of its analysed cells tracked on that frame whose MS2 signal is above the noise floor (the exported timecourse); onset -- the % of its analysed cells that have been above the floor on that frame or any before it.

6Segment histograms

Each segment's dashed box is stretched onto one square: centre (0, 0), right-upper corner (1, 1), left-lower (−1, −1), as seen in the turned view. The analysed cells inside the outline are binned at their average position over the window, and every sample's map of the same segment is shown side by side, then their mean.
