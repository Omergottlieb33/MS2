2Cells: tracking filters

A cell is analysed when it is tracked through enough of its stage window and its segmented volume is stable (volume CV = standard deviation over mean, over its tracked frames). Only these cells are shown when drawing the region and the segments below.

3Region and segments

One panel per sample, turned as set above, showing only the cells that pass the tracking filters. Choose what to draw, click around it, then close. ROI: outline the region on the start frame and on the end frame; a cell is analysed when it lies inside either outline on that frame (no outline: all of them). Each cell is outlined by its own segmentation on the frame shown: analysed cells in blue, the others faint grey. Nothing is drawn from earlier work unless you load an ROI or segment file for the sample. 2–5: outline each segment on the start frame, around the cells at their average position over the window. The dashed box is what the segment grid is cut from.

Box each segment

4Activity clustering

A cell fires on a frame when its MS2 signal is above the noise floor. Cells whose highest signal stays below the blip height are short bursts / noise; the rest are split into weakly, moderate and highly active by their rate (signal above the floor per tracked frame). Automatic places the two rate cuts where KMeans puts them on all samples' kept cells together; moving a cut takes over by hand.

Heatmap

Rows: every cell above silent, highest level on top (red lines between levels), then by sample, then by rate. Columns: frames from each window's start; grey where a cell is not tracked or its window has ended. Colour: signal on a log scale, the floor and below at the bottom.
