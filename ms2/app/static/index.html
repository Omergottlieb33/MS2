What this does

    
MS2 turns a live-imaging recording of an embryo (a Zeiss .czi with an MS2 channel
      and a cell channel) into how strongly each cell transcribes over time. Everything runs on the
      lab's GPU server; your browser only shows the page.

    
    
One of the lab's recordings through time, every step side by side: the raw cells,
      the segmentation, the tracks, the MS2 spots, and the cells grouped by activity.

    
The steps

    
    
      
  1. Preprocess: splits the two channels and removes the MS2 background.
    2. 
      
  2. Segment: finds every nucleus in 3D with Cellpose, frame by frame (the slow step, on the GPU).
    3. 
      
  3. Track: follows each cell from frame to frame.
    4. 
      
  4. Unify: splits cells that were merged and drops fragments, so each cell is one track.
    5. 
      
  5. Gene expression: finds the MS2 transcription spots and measures them in their cell over time.
    6. 
      
  6. Activity: groups the cells from silent to highly active.
    7. 
    

    
A finished step is kept: a stopped run picks up where it left off.

    
Using this page

    
    
      
  1. New analysis: choose a CZI and start. Every tracked cell is measured.
    2. 
      
  2. Runs & results: follow the steps and the log, open the viewer, and download the results.
    3. 
      
  3. Analysis: pick finished recordings, choose each stage's frames, the
        region, the cells, the activity levels and the segments, and compare them; save and export.
